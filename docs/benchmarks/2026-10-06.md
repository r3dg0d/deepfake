The old mask's clipped Gaussian had an abrupt boundary opacity step. The new mask uses inward smoothstep distance feathering, including a separate smooth transition around freshly parsed target mouth/lips. Original teeth, tongue and lip motion are protected rather than combined with differently shaped generated mouths. Normalized masked low-frequency lighting/chroma transfer excludes hidden pixels.

Near-profile samples already preserved much of the original face. Exposure clipping and extreme yaw remain limited by the existing backend; conservative fallback can retain an entire target face.
