# Security Policy

## Reporting

Report vulnerabilities privately. Do not attach illegal or CSAM-related materials.

## Product boundaries

- Consent acknowledgment is required for live/video runs (`--consent-ack` or `DEEPFAKE_CONSENT_ACK=1`).
- Synthetic-media watermark is on by default; disabling it does not remove your disclosure duties.
- Model downloads require explicit `--yes` and are checksum-recorded when possible.
- AlphaFace Drive weights are **not** redistributed by this project (license undocumented).
- InsightFace inswapper models are **non-commercial research** unless you hold a commercial license.
- This tool must not be used for non-consensual deepfakes, impersonation, fraud, or harassment.
